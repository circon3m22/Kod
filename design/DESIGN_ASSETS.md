## Промпты (смысловая запись)

1. Finished horizontal 16:9 cover for a Russian historical quiz, contemporary Swiss/editorial exhibition poster. Off-white background, black bold sans-serif title «КУЛЬТУРНЫЙ» / «КОД». Five distinct rounded photographic windows aligned in an asymmetrical modular grid around the title: medieval mounted warrior on a frozen lake; early-18th-century wooden Russian sailing ship launch; early-20th-century steam locomotive at a platform; construction of a steel truss railway bridge over a broad river; Soviet-style rocket launch. Ultra-photorealistic historical reconstructions, authentic materials and natural cinematic light. Harmonized muted steel-blue shadows and sand/taupe/copper highlights. No green, saturated accents, labels, dates, buttons, logos, gradients, glow, parchment or 3D UI.

2. Reflow the cover reference into a companion horizontal 4:3 poster. Preserve the exact Cyrillic title, the five realistic scene subjects, their color palette, flat rounded photo panels, consistent white gutters and outer margins. Reframe without stretching photos or typography. No added scenes, captions, dates, buttons, logos or watermarks.
